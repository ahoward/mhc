01 the idea

A special-ops team. Not an agency.

Not a shop you hand work to, and not a roster of names you'll never meet. A small unit of expert operators — each successful on their own — who embed with you and do the work themselves.

Every member has spent thirty years shipping real, production software — the kind that flies satellites, runs plants, moves fleets, and cannot fail. Scientific computing for the government. Infrastructure at scale. Systems that hold when they're loaded. Not decks, not demos — code in production.

You don't outsource and wait. You pair with operators and learn to run it yourself — working alongside private, bleeding-edge systems, not off-the-shelf tools with buttons and screens. The kind of person who'd build a cabin in Alaska or run a hundred miles: you want to understand every piece.

02 what we do

One unit. The whole build.

One team covers the full arc — so you're not stitching five vendors together, and no context leaks in the seams between them:

architecture build security & compliance data & integration delivery & ops design brand & copy positioning

Half a person of engineering and half a person of positioning beats two people of either. You book the mix the sprint actually needs — and every seat is a principal, not a hand-off.

03 the thesis

Context is the whole game now.

The edge in the new world is narrow and total: saying exactly the right thing, the right way, to the right models, at the right time. That is the job. The quality of the context is the quality of the software.

Which is why a game of telephone is now fatal. A nearshore team, an offshore team, a layer of account managers — every hop between the intent and the machine degrades the one thing that matters, at the exact moment in history when it matters most. You cannot delegate the context to people three time zones from the decision and expect the system to hold.

So the experts do it themselves. The person who understands your system is the person at the keyboard, talking to the models. That is context engineering — encoding the vision, the constraints, and the contracts so precisely that machines execute against them and validators prove it.

Humans declare intent. Machines generate implementations. Validators enforce contracts.

04 how we work

Quietly lethal.

A special-ops team, not a headcount. Small, senior, and moving at AI speeds — real software shipped in days and weeks, where a delegated shop would still be scoping.

The old VC clock — three-to-five-year vesting? LOL. Nobody knows what tools we'll be using in three months. Alpine style: no expedition of fifteen hauling a hundred pounds up the mountain. Trail runners, a shell, a clear line to the summit. Speedboat, not Titanic — small, fast, and dangerous.

Pair

Live sessions with an expert — ~2-hour blocks, several days a week. You both leave with tasks the AI runs down.

Sprint to a goal

Every engagement has one discrete target: ship the MVP, write the PRD, launch a region, first 100 customers.

Ship a repo

The deliverable is always a repository. GitHub is the backplane — perfect memory across every session and agent.

Own it

You keep everything — code, docs, the working MVP — yours to run or carry to the next firm.

05 the model

Ultra-simple economics.

A collective. Flat, distributed, lightweight. You book people in units — no one is ever beholden to a single client.

½person / month · the smallest thrust — research or an MVP
1–3units · scale up; ~40–60 hrs/week at full tilt
repoeverything ships to GitHub — yours to keep or take to the next firm

Paid half up front, half at the end. No exceptions, no overhead. Estimates in person-units and wallclock time — min and max. Proposals arrive as working MVPs.

07 who it's for

Systems that can't fail.

Real software for real operations — car makers, .gov and .mil, school systems, title monitoring, energy and mining, logistics. Regulated, load-bearing, consequential. Not the startup hype cycle — more marketing than meat — but the software an organization actually runs on.

A trucking company with 700 drivers that could build software, has never tried, and will lose if it doesn't. A cleared program that needs the work done inside the fence, by the people who understand it. We're the AI-tech partner for the things that matter — find the cutting-edge custom solution, build it, prove it, and — if they want — help them run a mini-SaaS off it. Like open source, but novel.
