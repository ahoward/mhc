## The frame: an engineering company (not a startup, not an agency)

Position as a **real software / engineering company**. Reject the word **"startup"** —
the hype cycle, "more marketing than meat." We still *launch things* (the team's
background is launching things — satellite systems to companies), but we call it
engineering, not startup theater.

## Aesthetic: NASA × North Face

Professional mountaineering **without the hippie/left read** — North Face, not
Patagonia (a touch less crunchy-green, slightly more corporate "ascend"). Combined
with **NASA**: mission-era engineering pride. Possibly **American colors** (open
question — weigh against the existing `mtⁿ` fireweed/glacier/sun palette).

Visual/communication language — **pocket-protector engineering**, not startup-slick:
- pocket protectors, schematics, invoices
- 3×5 cards, yellow sticky notes, whiteboards
- lightweight communication, rapid iteration
- monospace, mission-patch feel

## The thesis: meta-programming = AI programming ("specify things that specify things")

Blog-post seed (operator: "this is going to be the topic of a blog post"):
**"precisely specify things that specify other things"** — and even *future* things
(driving agents). AI-enabled programming **is** meta-programming. It demands an
accurate world model and **scientific rigor most developers cannot navigate**.

- If you like a **well-specified ticket with clear business goals from a stakeholder,
  you are irrelevant** in today's economy.
- The AI developer's job is **"to understand the understanding"** — the thought system.
  Generating valid code *requires* encoding a **worldview**: a way of knowing whether
  the software is correct.
- **Correctness is objective AND subjective.** It includes: does it harm children? will
  it make money? is it legal? is it "corporate enough"? will it appeal to millennials?
  Because of how LLMs work, **these words matter at every layer of the stack.** Encoding
  ethics/values at each step of AI usage **dramatically alters the output.** That is
  what engineering is now.
- **You don't need programmers anymore — you need scientists and true systems
  engineers** to model the **lexicon, concepts, and workflows** of your business, and
  from that a **custom worldview** (perhaps a fine-tuned model) that lets you both
  **generate** code and **validate** it is correct.
- Fits the existing pull-quote: *humans declare intent, machines generate
  implementations, validators enforce contracts* — now with **subjective** validators.

## How we work (reinforces anti-nearshore)

- **Daily 2-hour sessions**, hands-on, collaborative — we work the way we'll teach you.
- **Words matter:** "stateless" vs "stateful" absolutely changes the result. Precision

- This is exactly what sets us apart from **near-shore / obfuscated dark-factory shops**
  (in the pejorative sense) and the **game of telephone** — the context can't survive
  the hops.
